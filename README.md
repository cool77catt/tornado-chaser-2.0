# Tornado Chaser 2.0 🌪️🚗

Okay, so here's the deal: this project is an experiment. I want to see how far agentic AI can actually get building a real mobile game. Not a toy demo, but something you'd want to play on your phone. I'm steering the design, and an AI agent is doing most of the heavy lifting. Let's see how it goes.

## So what's the game?

You're a storm chaser. There's a tornado up ahead. Chase it.

It's an infinite runner, but with a twist. Most runners are about getting *away* from something. Here, you're trying to stay *close*, but not too close:

- **Get too close** and you get sucked up. Game over.
- **Fall too far behind** and you lose it. Also game over.
- **Stay in the pocket** and you rack up points. The closer you dare to get, the faster your score climbs.

Meanwhile the tornado is throwing stuff at you. Branches and trash cans will slow you down. A pickup truck falling out of the sky will end your run instantly. Dodge accordingly.

## The plan

The first goal is to nail the core loop: drive, dodge, chase, die, hit retry immediately. If that isn't fun, nothing else matters.

After that, the plan is:

- Unlockable and upgradable cars
- Missions and challenges
- Leaderboards
- Nastier tornadoes (fire tornado, anyone?)

## The nerdy stuff

- Built with **Unity 6** (URP)
- Made for **iOS and Android**, played in portrait mode with one thumb
